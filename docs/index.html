05 — WHERE THE DAYS GO

A late order is
a transit problem.

Split a late order's lead time into payment approval, seller handoff and carrier transit: 17 of the 19 extra days sit with the carrier. Handoff is the part sellers control — and it is the small part.

THE 19 EXTRA DAYS OF A LATE ORDER
Carrier transit+17.0 d
Payment approval + seller handoff+2.0 d
Boleto payments add a 29-hour approval lag and Friday handoffs run 2.5× slower — both absorbed entirely by the current buffer. Neither is where a late order is lost.

THE ASK

Three decisions,
in priority order.

01
Ship per-order promising at the 95th percentile. Same 21-day average promise, 47% fewer late deliveries, no change to operations.
02
Put the top of the seller queue on a handoff SLA. The first twelve sellers carry 2,000+ stars lost against their own benchmarks.
03
Track "never arrived" as its own metric. It is 17.7% of one-star reviews and invisible inside a late-rate target.
