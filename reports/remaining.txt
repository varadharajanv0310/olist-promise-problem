1              9686  37.71       16.69    -6.41  99.00  486.25    78.35

5             56855   3.02        9.23   -12.32  84.99  411.77    35.98

  => the operational gradient is SMOOTH and monotonic across all five scores - late rate
     37.7 / 20.7 / 11.1 / 5.0 / 3.0%, delivery time 16.7 / 13.3 / 12.0 / 10.8 / 9.2 days.
     Even the 3-to-4 step, which looks like a matter of taste, is a 2.2x difference in
     lateness. The review score is not a blunt satisfaction proxy: on this platform it is
     close to a continuous readout of delivery performance.
     Order value and distance, by contrast, are flat across the whole scale.
