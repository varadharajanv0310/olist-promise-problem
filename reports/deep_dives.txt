2. FREIGHT vs PRODUCT CHARACTERISTICS - what is Olist actually charging for?
==========================================================================================
  single-item orders analysed: 88,100

  correlation with freight charged:
    weight (log)         r = +0.536
    volume (log)         r = +0.446
    distance (log)       r = +0.581
    order value (log)    r = +0.470

  log-log model  log(freight) ~ log(weight) + log(volume) + log(distance)
    weight elasticity   +0.159   (a 10% heavier parcel costs +1.59% more)
    volume elasticity   +0.031
    distance elasticity +0.194   (a 10% longer trip costs +1.94% more)
    R2 = 0.600

  => BOTH elasticities are far below 1: a 10x longer trip raises freight only 1.56x,
     and a 10x heavier parcel only 1.44x. Freight is heavily compressed against
     real cost, so long-haul orders are cross-subsidised by short-haul ones.
